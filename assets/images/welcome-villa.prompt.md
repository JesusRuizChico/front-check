# Welcome villa image

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, using the user's welcome-screen mockup as a visual reference. Buttons, location pin and curved shapes are rendered in Flutter.

## Prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: photographic hero asset for a Flutter real estate welcome screen.
Input image: the attached phone mockup is a composition and architecture reference only.
Generate a NEW clean photorealistic architectural photograph of the same style of contemporary two-story Mediterranean villa: ivory stucco rectangular volumes, warm honey oak vertical and horizontal cladding, tall black-framed glass windows, clear glass balcony, soft warm interiors, a mature olive tree dominating the left foreground, shrubs and pale stone entry steps at the bottom. Warm afternoon light. Match the reference's camera angle, with the prominent front upper roof corner around 62% across the frame and 23% down, and the right wall extending out of frame. Building occupies the central and right two thirds. Pale muted blue cloudless sky in the upper quarter. Elegant, natural, credible materials.
Composition: portrait 4:5 photograph, edge to edge, enough garden at the bottom and sky above; no UI layout. The photo will be cropped into a curved shape in code.
Constraints: NO phone, NO phone bezel, NO borders, NO text, NO buttons, NO logos, NO location pin, NO graphic circles or overlays, NO people. Only the house, plants, entry and sky. High detail, realistic architectural photography.
